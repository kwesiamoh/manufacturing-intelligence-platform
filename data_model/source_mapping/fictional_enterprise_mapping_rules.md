# Fictional Enterprise Mapping Rules

1. The six enterprise sites and 30 production lines are synthetic integration entities required by the project brief.
2. They must always be marked `FICTIONAL_ENTERPRISE` and `SYNTHETIC_INTEGRATION`.
3. Real public datasets retain their original source identities.
4. A real dataset must not be relabelled as if it was measured at one of these fictional sites.
5. Cross-source combinations are allowed only in explicitly synthetic demonstration or benchmark layers.
6. The coordinates are representative city-level locations used for ERA5-Land weather context. They are not claimed factory addresses.
7. No production capacity, product mix, OEE, energy use, maintenance history, or financial values are created in Stage 2E.
