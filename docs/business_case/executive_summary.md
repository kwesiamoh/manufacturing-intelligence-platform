# Executive Roadmap & Business Case Summary

The Manufacturing Intelligence Platform demonstrates a governed end-to-end
manufacturing analytics architecture spanning data ingestion, enterprise
integration, KPI reporting, advanced analytics, reliability, and condition
monitoring.

For the full 2024-2025 two-year period, the synthetic enterprise model
identifies **€573,908,429.76** (approximately **€573.91M**) of modeled technical
opportunity across six sites. Its simple annualized technical-opportunity base
is **€286,954,214.88 per year** (approximately **€286.95M/year**).

These figures are modeled prioritization signals. The annualized base is a
simple two-year average, not a forecast, and neither figure is committed or
realized financial savings.

A real implementation should begin with a controlled plant pilot focused on
production/OEE, data quality, and one or two actionable loss use cases. Energy
and maintenance can then be added as source-system integration matures.

Financial approval should be based on:

- validated real-plant baselines;
- mutually exclusive benefit calculations;
- documented implementation and operating costs;
- named business owners;
- measured post-intervention improvement.

The technical platform is designed to be AWS-ready, but the portfolio contains
no live AWS deployment and maintains a hard €0 cloud-spend constraint.

The recommended program sequence is:

1. controlled plant pilot;
2. operational use-case scale-up;
3. multi-site standardization;
4. production platform engineering;
5. advanced decision support only where measurable value is demonstrated.
